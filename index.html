The Ritual

Four minutes.
Every morning.

I
Cleanse — Slowly

Begin with cool water and a small amount of the Morning Cleanser emulsified in your palms. The oil-to-milk transformation as you rinse is intentional — it signals the transition between sleep and attention. Take thirty seconds longer than you think you need.

Morning Cleanser
II
Treat — While Still Damp

The Luminance Serum absorbs best into skin that retains a trace of moisture. Three drops across cheekbones, forehead, and chin. Press — do not rub — until the serum disappears into the skin rather than sitting on top of it. Give it ninety seconds to settle before the next step.

Luminance Serum
III
Seal — With the Oil

Two drops of the Dusk Face Oil into the cup of your palm, warmed briefly. Pressed over the serum to lock in moisture and activate the actives. The scent — faint, botanical, nothing synthetic — is half the point. It takes three seconds. It changes the rest of the morning.

Dusk Face Oil
IV
Rest — Overnight

On the evenings your skin needs more — and it will tell you — a small amount of the Overnight Balm pressed into the most vulnerable areas: around the eyes, the corners of the mouth, the parts that hold the expression of the day. By morning, the difference is visible.

Overnight Balm
The Ingredients

What's inside
matters.

Every botanical in the Veldt collection is cold-pressed, ethically sourced, and included at concentrations that actually work. We list our percentages — most brands don't.

🌹
Rosehip
Chilean Andes · Cold-Pressed

The highest natural concentration of Vitamin C available in plant form, alongside Vitamin A and essential fatty acids. Used at 18% in the Luminance Serum — six times the industry standard.

🌊
Sea Buckthorn
Himalayan Foothills · First Press

The deepest orange in botanical skincare, dense with carotenoids, omega-7, and a Vitamin E complex that repairs barrier function at the cellular level. A trace amount colors the entire formula.

🌿
Squalane
Sugarcane-derived · Fermented

Plant-derived squalane mirrors the skin's own natural lipid profile so precisely that it absorbs without a trace of residue. The base of the Overnight Balm. The reason it disappears by morning.

🌸
Calendula CO₂
Southern California · Supercritical Extract

Grown on a small farm in San Diego County. Extracted via supercritical CO₂ to preserve the full phytochemical profile. Anti-inflammatory, barrier-strengthening, and the ingredient most responsible for the Cleanser's gentleness.

🫒
Jojoba
Sonoran Desert · Single-Origin

Technically a wax ester, not an oil, which means it behaves more like the skin's own sebum than any other botanical. Regulates without stripping. Hydrates without clogging. The silent base of the Face Oil.

🌻
Vitamin C Complex
L-Ascorbic Acid · 23% · Stabilized

The most potent form of Vitamin C available, stabilized in an anhydrous base at a pH that allows actual penetration. Most Vitamin C products are below 10% and ineffective at their pH. Ours is neither.
